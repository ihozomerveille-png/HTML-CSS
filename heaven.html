About Heaven Restaurant

Heaven Restaurant is one of Kigali's most iconic dining destinations. Perched on a hilltop in the Kiyovu neighborhood, it offers stunning panoramic views of the city alongside a menu that blends international cuisine with local Rwandan flavors.

Visit their official site: heavenrwanda.com
