About the Memorial

The Kigali Genocide Memorial is the final resting place for more than 250,000 victims of the 1994 Genocide against the Tutsi. It serves as both a burial site and an educational museum that documents the history of the genocide.

Learn more at the official Kigali Genocide Memorial website.

What to Expect

  • Outdoor memorial gardens and burial sites
  • Indoor museum with historical exhibits
  • Children's memorial room
  • Guided tours available
  • Gift shop and bookstore

Visitor Guidelines

  1. Dress respectfully — no shorts or sleeveless tops
  2. Photography is restricted inside the museum
  3. Maintain silence in memorial areas
  4. Children under 13 require parental guidance
  5. Free entry — donations welcome
