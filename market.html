About Kimironko Market

Kimironko Market is one of the largest and busiest markets in Kigali. Located in the Gasabo District, it is a hub for fresh produce, clothing, crafts, electronics, and local street food. It's the perfect place to experience everyday Rwandan life.

Read more about Kigali markets on Wikipedia.

Market Scenes

What You Can Buy

  • Fresh fruits and vegetables
  • Traditional Rwandan fabrics (Kitenge)
  • Handmade crafts and souvenirs
  • Second-hand clothing (Chagua)
  • Spices and local ingredients
  • Electronics and household items

Tips for Visiting

  1. Go early in the morning for the freshest produce
  2. Bargaining is expected — start at half the asking price
  3. Keep your belongings secure in crowded areas
  4. Try the local street food stalls inside the market
  5. Bring cash — most vendors don't accept cards
