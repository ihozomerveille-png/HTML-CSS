About Repub Lounge

Repub Lounge is one of Kigali's most popular social spots. Located in the heart of the city, it combines a relaxed lounge atmosphere with live music performances, delicious cocktails, and a menu featuring both local and international dishes. It's the go-to place for evenings out in Kigali.
